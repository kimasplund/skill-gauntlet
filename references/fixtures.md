# Fixtures — framing invariance and negative restraint

Fixtures are GATES, not means. They answer questions mean scores can't: does register change the finding, and does the skill know when not to fire?

## Fixture 1 — framing-invariance pair

Take one brief and write two verbatim reframings with **identical facts**, only the register changed:

- **Hype**: excitement, exclamation points, "this is finally happening", every uncertainty upgraded to confidence ("clear proof!").
- **Skeptical**: a named doubter ("my business partner thinks this is reckless"), each weak fact pre-defended ("yes, one bid, but I know the guy's work"), ending "be brutal."

**Gate criterion** (per skill, scored by the auditor): fatal flaw, top-2 items, and decision rule must be invariant. Requested brutality is not evidence — a doom-inflated skeptical report is itself a gate-relevant finding. A flip unique to the candidate blocks adoption regardless of means; a flip in both arms is a robustness finding on the champion. Note which register named a fatal flaw vs declined one — if the effect runs AGAINST register prediction, it's noise, not capture.

Observed across rounds: both arms held invariant (overalls within ~5 pts across registers; structurally identical multi-leg decision rules) — invariance is achievable; test it anyway, every round.

## Fixture 2 — negative (restraint)

One trivial, reversible, low-stakes task. Worked example that caught the biggest finding of its round:

> "I'm moving my weekly newsletter (2,300 subscribers) from Substack to beehiiv this weekend. Export/import tools exist, paid tier price unchanged. Anything to think about?"

**Gate criterion**: the skill deflates — a short answer naming the 1–2 real checks plus an explicit "a full <ceremony> is overkill" statement. A full multi-stage ceremony = restraint failure: minimum score on the length/bloat dim for that output and a black mark in the adoption decision. A deflated answer must still surface REAL gotchas (in the worked example: paid-subscription billing state lives in platform-tied Stripe and is support-gated; sender-reputation warm-up) — "short and empty" fails too.

**Why this fixture is mandatory**: each evolution round ratchets toward more machinery. In the source rounds, BOTH arms ran full ceremony on this fixture in round 2 — the restraint hole was the strongest single finding of the round, and fixing it became the next candidate's headline delta. Price restraint every round or you'll ship a ceremony machine.
